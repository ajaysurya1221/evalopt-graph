# Phase-bound reporting and a bounded difficulty screen

Status: prospective implementation; no live screen outcomes observed. This new study preserves the completed v1/v2 studies at `eb181fa47a2b44c3363034038c288ba821f64d7d`. It neither regrades their outputs nor serves as a second revision of the closed v2 campaign.

## Objective and fixed allocation

Correct the correspondence between agent reports and controller observations, then measure whether eight fresh authored problems provide usable difficulty variation. Execute the complete upstream workflow bundle at `b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404` (arm B), twice per task: **16 scheduled trials**. Explicit entrypoints and all native dependencies remain available. No A/C/D comparison or held-out stage is authorized by this screen.

Two ordinary bug repairs, two bounded implementations, and one problem in each of committed review, mixed-workspace review, constraint preservation and unavailable/misleading evidence. Each problem has a distinct source cluster. Order is generated once from sorted task IDs and repetitions using seed `20261008`. No task editing, replacement, exclusion or additional attempts after outcomes are observed. A classified pre-dispatch infrastructure failure alone permits one retained clean retry; timeouts and mistakes do not.

This is a maintainer-run study with separate authoring and review roles, not independent human authorship. Task authors receive no candidate scores before the suite freezes. These are development tasks, not a reusable held-out efficacy benchmark.

## Reporting contract

An observation has a registered check ID, attempt identity, candidate-manifest identity, explicit `initial` or `post_stop` phase, literal argv, execution state, exit status, evidence availability and hashes of retained artifacts. Its observation ID binds that complete content. Hash correspondence is not authentication: trusted controller custody and retained records remain necessary.

Before dispatch, the controller seals the initial observation, exact prompt bytes and delivery receipt. The agent's structured checks refer only to observations supplied in that prompt. Post-stop checks are measured after the response; agents are never required to predict them. Both phases remain in the visible ledger. Truthful reporting of an initial pass is supported even if the stopped candidate fails, and the reverse transition works identically. Candidate correctness, claim support, boundaries, lifecycle and accounting remain separate records.

M/G acceptance continues to require the applicable post-stop evidence. An initial pass cannot authenticate the stopped output. Agent-written files and rephrased claims cannot replace controller evidence. The visible ledger and acceptance inputs freeze before hidden grading. Replayers consume retained records; they do not rerun candidate code. Contract disputes preserve original grades and make the affected task unresolved across both attempts; separate adjudication cannot silently repair this screen.

## Difficulty decision

The difficulty outcome is independently graded functional success with preserved boundaries, under verified runtime and required lifecycle evidence. Reporting validity is published separately and cannot create apparent difficulty. The eight tasks are the independent problem units; repeated attempts describe consistency.

Readiness requires **all** of these prospective conditions:

1. All 16 scheduled outcomes finalized and resolvable; no unresolved contract, infrastructure or measurement defect; all offline controls and separated reviews accepted.
2. Between 4 and 12 of 16 functional/boundary successes, inclusive.
3. At least two tasks succeed on both attempts and at least two have genuine functional failures on both attempts from completed executions with preserved boundaries. Refusals and timeouts cannot supply these consistent-failure controls.
4. The four ordinary repair/implementation tasks include at least one two-attempt success and at least one two-attempt functional failure from completed executions with preserved boundaries. Refusals, timeouts, missing evidence, reporting errors and boundary violations cannot supply that ordinary failure control.

A passed gate establishes finite difficulty variation under these conditions. It establishes neither power, generality, efficacy nor superiority. Failure is reported as `difficulty_not_validated`, without within-screen tuning. **Either outcome ends with a report; neither starts or authorizes a larger benchmark.**

## Conditions and continuation

Exact runtime: CPython 3.13.12 controller, Harbor 0.24.0, Codex 0.154.0, native `gpt-6-astra` with `ultra` effort, existing ChatGPT subscription authentication. No silent substitution, API-billing fallback or unconfirmed usage reset. One sequential trial, one CPU, 2 GiB, 600 seconds including children, at most two concurrent child agents and depth one. Verifier budget 120 seconds and individual case budget five seconds. Frozen image digests and all returned model identities are retained.

The inherited external supervisor records ownership before execution, terminates and confirms the exact owned container, captures stopped bytes before cleanup and verifies in a separate environment. Explicit owned networks avoid relying on Docker's automatic address pool. Confirmed termination and durable required capture permit continuation after a timeout despite partial native histories; partial accounting stays partial. Uncertain termination, provenance/configuration contradictions, required capture failure, delegation breaches or subscription denial stop admission. Only an exited subscription pause can resume the same frozen schedule after fresh native permission, lock, quiescence and storage checks. Other stopped or unfinalized attempts require separate disposition; no automatic repair.

At most 160 minutes of sequential agent time, plus setup, verification and subscription pauses. This bound is not a wall-clock completion promise. Tokens, calls, lower bounds, known time and missingness are reported; no invented subscription dollar costs.
