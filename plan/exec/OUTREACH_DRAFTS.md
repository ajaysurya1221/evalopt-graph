## Closure announcement

Standalone feature development for evalopt has ended. The repository preserves the acceptance kernel and its research record. Migration guidance routes new systems to established policy, provenance and native enforcement tools while identifying what does not map automatically. Evalopt evaluates supplied evidence against policy; it is not a semantic truth oracle. Existing evidence retains its original versions and limitations. See the repository notice and migration guide: https://github.com/ajaysurya1221/evalopt-graph and https://github.com/ajaysurya1221/evalopt-graph/blob/main/docs/migration.md .

## Standards clarification

We are closing a small standalone policy-evidence experiment and documenting migration rather than adding another standard or adapter. Our guide distinguishes content-hash binding from producer authentication, policy evaluation from semantic correctness, and project-specific acceptance states from attestation envelopes. It treats SLSA VSA 1.0 as retired historical prior art, not a current migration target. If a specific mapping in the guide misstates your published contract, a source-linked correction would help existing readers: https://github.com/ajaysurya1221/evalopt-graph/blob/main/docs/migration.md . This is a request to clarify documentation, not an endorsement or integration request.
